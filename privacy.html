Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Daily Read is a free app for iPhone and iPad for reading and listening to Bible books, Christian classics, a daily devotional and short learning Tracks. It is made by Nino Moscardini, an individual developer.

The short version

Daily Read does not collect any information about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What stays on your device

The app stores these things on your iPhone or iPad so it can work:

  • the library of books, readings and Tracks;
  • your thoughts, favourites and reading history;
  • your settings, such as books per day and voice;
  • your progress through the devotional and Tracks.

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. The developer cannot see it.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, your device may include Daily Read's data in that backup, as it does for most apps. That is handled by Apple and your own settings, not by Daily Read.

Listening

Spoken readings use voices built into your device by Apple. The text is spoken on the device. Daily Read does not send it anywhere.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use Siri or Shortcuts with Daily Read (for example "Play today's Daily Read"), Apple handles the voice request under Apple's own privacy policy. Daily Read only receives the command.

Importing a library

You can choose to import a library file that someone sends you, by AirDrop, Messages, Mail or the Files app. The file is read on your device. Daily Read keeps a backup before importing and keeps your own thoughts, favourites and reading history. Daily Read never downloads anything by itself.

If you share a file from Daily Read, you choose who receives it, using Apple's share sheet.

Open-source software

Daily Read includes GRDB, an open-source database library that runs on your device. It sends nothing anywhere.

Children

Daily Read does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date. If Daily Read ever starts collecting information, the policy will say so clearly before the app does.
