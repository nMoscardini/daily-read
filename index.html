Daily Read

Daily Read is a free app for iPhone and iPad. Each day it offers a short devotional, a few ideas from Bible books and Christian classics, and the next part of a learning Track. You can read or listen.

Support

For questions, problems or suggestions, email dailyread.always207@passfwd.com.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-in, and the app works offline.

What is Import Library? Settings > Library > Import Library adds a library file someone has sent you. Your own thoughts, favourites and reading history are kept.

How many books a day? Choose in Settings > Today.

Where is my data? On your device only. See the privacy policy.
